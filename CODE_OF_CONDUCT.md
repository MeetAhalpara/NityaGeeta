# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the spirit of *Satya* (truthfulness), *Ahimsa* (non-harm), and *Samatvam* (equanimity), we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **NityaGeeta** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or spiritual tradition.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and reverent community dedicated to preserving and exploring timeless philosophical wisdom.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:
* **Reverence for Scripture & Truth**: Treating sacred texts, commentaries, and scholarly debates with respect and intellectual rigor.
* **Demonstrating Empathy & Kindness**: Being respectful of differing viewpoints and spiritual interpretations across diverse traditions (*Sampradayas*).
* **Gracefully Accepting Constructive Criticism**: Welcoming corrections to OCR typos, Sanskrit transliterations, and algorithm accuracy.
* **Focusing on What Is Best for the Community**: Prioritizing open educational access, factual accuracy, and user safety over ego.
* **Showing Respect to Fellow Contributors**: Encouraging new contributors and maintaining professional, courteous dialogue.

### Unacceptable Behavior
Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Disrespectful mockery or intentional distortion of sacred Sanskrit verses or traditional commentators (*Acharyas*).
* Harassment, public or private, in any form.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative scholarly and open-source environment.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at:
* **Email**: [contact@nityageeta.org](mailto:contact@nityageeta.org)
* **Website Portal**: [NityaGeeta Contact & Feedback](https://nityageeta.org/contact)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
