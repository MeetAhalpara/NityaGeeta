# Accessibility Statement for NityaGeeta

NityaGeeta is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including seekers, scholars, and individuals with disabilities. We continually strive to improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards.

## Conformance Status
We target compliance with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** standards across the NityaGeeta platform.

## Measures to Support Accessibility
- **High-Contrast Theme Tokens:** Supporting both Light (`#FAF7F2`) and Dark (`#1E1B18`) modes with high-contrast scripture text and legible terracotta accents (`#C25E38`).
- **Semantic HTML & ARIA:** Utilizing native HTML5 semantic elements (`<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<section>`) and descriptive ARIA labels on dynamic components (drawers, modals, search bars).
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Ensuring full focus management, visible focus rings, and keyboard operability for interactive dialogs and Sanskrit commentary drawers.
- **Screen Reader Support:** Providing alternative text for images, diagrams, and iconography (Lucide icon wrappers with accessible descriptions).
- **Reduced Motion Support:** Honoring `prefers-reduced-motion` settings in Framer Motion animations to prevent vestibular triggers.

## Known Limitations & Alternative Formats
While we aim for full accessibility, complex ancient manuscript scans (such as historical PDF facsimile pages) may have limitations with automated screen readers. To address this:
- We provide digitized, structured Sanskrit Unicode text and English translations alongside all manuscript facsimile displays.

## Feedback & Reporting Accessibility Barriers
We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of NityaGeeta. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please open an issue using our [Bug Report template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md) or contact us via our [Contact Page](https://github.com/MeetAhalpara/NityaGeeta).
